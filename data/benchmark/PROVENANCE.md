## Pre-Screening & Zero-Solver Firewall Protocol (`Q1`)
1. **Disjointness & Homology Screen**: All public BMRB entries with `timedomain_data/` archives were filtered to remove every BMRB accession referenced anywhere in the repository (`21` empirical-prior entries in `data/priors/PROVENANCE.md`, `12` Phase-1 peak-list entries in `data/benchmark/manifest.tsv`, all `19` prior `real_manifest.tsv` entries, and all unit/integration test fixtures), plus entries homologous to Ubiquitin/GB1/NifU-NFU1/CISD families or marked perdeuterated (`2H`).
2. **NMR-STAR Metadata Screen**: Candidate `.str` headers were checked strictly for:
   - Single monomeric polypeptide chain (`50 <= L <= 160` canonical amino acids; oligomers, complexes, membrane/micelle samples, IDPs, and paramagnetic metalloproteins excluded),
   - `>= 80%` non-Proline `1H`/`15N` and `>= 80%` `13CA` deposited backbone chemical shifts in the primary `_Atom_chem_shift` loop (`>= 70%` `13CB` or `13C`).
3. **Header-Only Archive Verification (`ZERO` FFT / Peak-Picker / Solver Runs)**:
   - Candidate archives (`<= 650 MB`) were fetched via `nmrassign fetch-bmrb` and inspected **solely** via `nmrassign inspect --json` and `discover_entry_experiments` (`acqus` / `procpar` text headers only) to confirm the presence of a raw time-domain `2D 15N-HSQC` plus `>= 3` raw time-domain 3D triple-resonance backbone experiments (`HNCO`, `HNCA`, `HNCACB`, `CBCACONH`, `HNCOCA`, `HNCACO`). Archives containing only NOESY or 2D FIDs (`bmr25061`, `bmr16100`, `bmr16272`, `bmr25067`, `bmr17965`, `bmr18511`) or lacking discoverable 3D `HNCO` headers (`bmr5589`) were discarded at header inspection without ever invoking FFT processing, peak picking, or assignment.

## Calibration Scope & Hardware Provenance Disclosure (`P1-7`, `P1-8`, `Q2` & `Q4`)
- **Replica-count coupling, conservative `Beta` priors & nested LOPO shrinkage (`P1-8`)**: `STANDARD_CALIBRATION_KNOTS` is fitted at `runs = 16` (`STANDARD_CPU_CALIBRATED_RUNS`) using conservative conjugate `Beta(0.75, 2.25)` PAV (`STANDARD_CPU_PRIOR_ALPHA = 0.75`, `STANDARD_CPU_PRIOR_BETA = 2.25`), `STANDARD_GPU_CALIBRATION_KNOTS` at `runs = 64` (`STANDARD_GPU_CALIBRATED_RUNS`) on **`Apple M5 Pro` (`Metal` backend via `wgpu`, `f32` WGSL compute)** using mild conservative `Beta(0.35, 0.65)` PAV (`STANDARD_GPU_PRIOR_ALPHA = 0.35`, `STANDARD_GPU_PRIOR_BETA = 0.65`) with empirical-Bayes shrinkage toward CPU `f64` bin counts (`CANONICAL_GPU_CPU_SHRINKAGE_WEIGHT = 0.50`, selected via full-13 `dev` inner LOPO over `CANDIDATE_GPU_SHRINKAGE_WEIGHTS`, while outer OOF generalization is evaluated by **nested** 13x12 LOPO selecting `w_i` strictly inside the 12 training proteins of each outer fold `i`), and `EVO_GRAPH_CALIBRATION_KNOTS` at `population_size = 64` (`EVO_GRAPH_CALIBRATED_RUNS`) using symmetric `Beta(0.25, 0.25)` PAV. Callers overriding `runs != fit_runs` emit a `stderr` warning and set `DatasetQcReport.confidence_calibrated = false`.
- **Primary `ECE_10` gate & finite-sample Bernoulli floor (`P1-8`)**: Pooled 10-bin Expected Calibration Error (`pooled_ECE_10 <= 0.050` on `UNIFORM_CALIBRATION_GRID_10`) and micro high-confidence precision (`>= 94.0%`, macro `>= 95.0%`) serve as the primary calibration gates alongside `ECE_4`, `ECE_10`, and `ECE_12`. Because unpooled 10-bin plug-in `ECE_10` on single `N = 32–102` residue proteins has an analytical Half-Normal Bernoulli floor (`ece_10_bernoulli_floor ~ 0.067–0.095`) even under an exact oracle, both `ece_10_bernoulli_floor` and Ferro–Fricker (2012) variance-debiased `debiased_ece_10` (`<= 0.065`) are reported in JSON and text outputs.
- **Unified in-fold protein-level HC gate, fixed structural specification constants, `Q4` sub-HC uncalibrated metadata & spent `seen_p12` notice (`P1-7`, `Q4`)**: In Phase 13, a single unified `ProteinHcGateConfig` (`CANONICAL_PROTEIN_HC_GATE = { min_corroborated_connectivity: 0.45, min_hc_call_fraction: 0.04, min_crowded_solution_margin: 0.25, multiplicity_penalty_floor: 0.06 }`) and isotonic PAV knots are refit together across all 3 engines inside every 13-fold `dev` LOPO training split (`fit_protein_hc_gate_config_multi_engine`) over a broad un-peeked 400-point 4D product grid (`[0.45..0.65] x [0.04..0.12] x [0.25..0.55] x [0.06..0.12]`) with a neutral monotone Occam tie-breaker (`1e-4 * sum_of_thresholds`, preferring the least restrictive gate whenever training `ECE_10` and `HCP >= 94.5%` tie) and zero outer-fold leakage. Fixed structural specification constants (`QC_CORROBORATED_PAIR_THRESHOLD = 3.5` nats, `QC_MIN_LARGE_OFFSET_GAIN_PER_OBS = 0.15` nats/obs for `|dCA|, |dCB| > 3.20` ppm and `0.50 * 0.15 = 0.075` nats/obs for `|dN| > 1.50` ppm on Pass-1 unlabelled fits, and `is_unconverged_solution_margin` `(0.65, 0.07, 1.60, 0.16)`) apply uniformly across all folds. Under `DatasetQcVerdict::Warn`, `warn_sub_hc_uncalibrated = true` (`Q4`) explicitly marks sub-HC calls (`< 0.80`) as uncalibrated rather than fitting a noisy separate per-tier PAV map on tiny `Warn` sample counts. Having been evaluated once at `v0.8.0`, former `cold_p12` entries are relabeled `seen_p12` (`heldout`) and never touched during Phase 13 design or fitting; a fresh `cold_p13` cohort will be fetched and pre-registered after full Phase 13 code freeze.
