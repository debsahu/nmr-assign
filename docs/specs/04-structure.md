## 1. Torsion-Space Polymer Kinematics & Iterative ADR Folding (`noesy.rs`, `structure.rs`)

### 1.1 Rigid Covalent Geometry & `NeRF` Reconstruction
Every conformer of length $L$ is parameterized by internal backbone torsions $(\phi_i, \psi_i, \omega_i)_{i=0}^{L-1}$ with rigid Engh & Huber covalent bond lengths ($r_{\text{N–C}^\alpha} = 1.458\text{ \AA}$, $r_{\text{C}^\alpha\text{–C}'} = 1.525\text{ \AA}$, $r_{\text{C}'\text{–N}} = 1.329\text{ \AA}$, $r_{\text{C}'\text{–O}} = 1.231\text{ \AA}$, $r_{\text{N–H}} = 1.010\text{ \AA}$) and interior bond angles. Chiral L-amino acid $\text{C}^\beta$, $\text{H}^\alpha$, and $\text{Q}^\beta$ pseudoatom coordinates are reconstructed deterministically from $(\text{N}_i, \text{C}^\alpha_i, \text{C}'_i)$ so they rotate rigidly with segment $2i + 1$ around the $\text{N}_i \to \text{C}^\alpha_i$ ($\phi_i$) axis.

### 1.2 Exact $O(L)$ Analytical Torsion Gradients
Given Cartesian energy gradients $\mathbf{g}_a = \partial E / \partial \mathbf{r}_a$ accumulated onto rigid polymer segments $s \in \{0, \dots, 2L\}$ ($\text{N}_i, \text{H}_i, \text{C}^\alpha_i \in 2i$; $\text{C}'_i, \text{H}^\alpha_i, \text{C}^\beta_i \in 2i + 1$; $\text{O}_i \in 2i + 2$), the backward suffix sums

$$\mathbf{F}_t = \sum_{s > t} \sum_{a \in s} \mathbf{g}_a, \qquad \mathbf{T}_t = \sum_{s > t} \sum_{a \in s} (\mathbf{r}_a \times \mathbf{g}_a)$$

yield exact analytical torsion derivatives in $O(L)$ operations:

$$\frac{\partial E}{\partial \theta_t} = \hat{\mathbf{e}}_t \cdot \left(\mathbf{T}_t - \mathbf{p}_t \times \mathbf{F}_t\right)$$

where $(\hat{\mathbf{e}}_{2i}, \mathbf{p}_{2i}) = (\widehat{\mathbf{r}_{\text{C}^\alpha_i} - \mathbf{r}_{\text{N}_i}}, \mathbf{r}_{\text{C}^\alpha_i})$ for $\phi_i$ and $(\hat{\mathbf{e}}_{2i+1}, \mathbf{p}_{2i+1}) = (\widehat{\mathbf{r}_{\text{C}'_i} - \mathbf{r}_{\text{C}^\alpha_i}}, \mathbf{r}_{\text{C}'_i})$ for $\psi_i$. Subtracting carbonyl oxygen $\text{O}_i$'s local force and torque $(\mathbf{f}_{\text{O}_i}, \mathbf{r}_{\text{O}_i} \times \mathbf{f}_{\text{O}_i})$ from $(\mathbf{F}_{2i+1}, \mathbf{T}_{2i+1})$ simultaneously yields exact $O(L)$ peptide-bond $\partial E / \partial \omega_i$ derivatives around $(\hat{\mathbf{e}}_{\omega_i}, \mathbf{p}_{\omega_i}) = (\widehat{\mathbf{r}_{\text{N}_{i+1}} - \mathbf{r}_{\text{C}'_i}}, \mathbf{r}_{\text{N}_{i+1}})$.

### 1.3 Multi-Cycle Ambiguous NOESY Folding
1. **Initial matching & network anchoring**: 3D ${}^{15}\text{N}$-edited and ${}^{13}\text{C}$-edited NOESY crosspeaks are matched to assigned $(\text{H}^{\text{N}}, \text{H}^\alpha, \text{Q}^\beta)$ resonances and scored by covalent, sequential, reciprocal, and triangle contact consistency.
2. **Three-stage torsion annealing**: Parallel replicas undergo high-temperature soft-core Langevin dynamics (Stage 1), steric excluded-volume inflation and cooling (Stage 2), and full-potential two-loop `L-BFGS` minimization (Stage 3).
3. **Iterative `<d^-6>` ADR filtering**: Across refinement cycles, candidate weights are updated from ensemble distances via $w_k \propto \langle d_k^{-6} \rangle$, promoting dominant contacts ($\ge 85\%$ weight) to unambiguous distance restraints.

### 2.2 Non-Redundant `CC0` Fragment Corpus (`data/torsion_db/torsion_corpus.tsv` & `scripts/build_torsion_corpus.py`)

### 2.3 Unlabelled Global Chemical-Shift Offset Estimation
Before fragment scoring, `estimate_global_shift_offsets` separates spectrometer referencing offsets (e.g., `TMS` vs `DSS` $\approx \pm 2\text{ ppm}$ on ${}^{13}\text{C}$, liquid $\text{NH}_3$ offsets on ${}^{15}\text{N}$) from secondary-structure chemical-shift polarization using ZERO ground-truth labels:
1. A 3-state `(Coil, Helix, Sheet)` expectation-maximization (`EM`) decomposition exploits the opposite signs of $\Delta\delta_{\text{C}^\alpha}$ and $\Delta\delta_{\text{C}^\beta}$ in $\alpha$-helices versus $\beta$-sheets while fitting a shared uniform reference offset $(\delta_0^{\text{H}}, \delta_0^{\text{N}}, \delta_0^{\text{C}^\alpha}, \delta_0^{\text{C}^\beta}, \delta_0^{\text{C}'}, \delta_0^{\text{H}^\alpha})$.
2. Per-nucleus soft deadzones ($0.32\,\sigma_k$, with $\boldsymbol{\sigma} = [0.42, 3.20, 1.35, 1.45, 1.25, 0.28]\text{ ppm}$ for $(\text{H}, \text{N}, \text{C}^\alpha, \text{C}^\beta, \text{C}', \text{H}^\alpha)$) suppress spurious drift on small unbalanced single-motif domains while accurately recovering genuine mis-referencing offsets.

### 2.4 Hybrid 3-mer + 9-mer Fragment Scoring & Residue Classification
1. **Flexibility gate (`RCI` $S^2$)**: Smoothed 6-nucleus Berjanskii–Wishart Random Coil Index (`RCI`) and order parameter $S^2_{\text{RCI}}$ flag intrinsically disordered and flexible loop residues ($S^2_{\text{RCI}} < 0.65$) as `Dynamic` prior to restraint emission.
2. **Peptide-bond-aware 3-mer + 9-mer dissimilarity**: Every contiguous database window matching exact `Gly` / `Pro` / `Pre-Pro` center identity is scored by combining secondary-shift $z$-squared residuals across $(\Delta\delta_{\text{H}}, \Delta\delta_{\text{N}}, \Delta\delta_{\text{C}^\alpha}, \Delta\delta_{\text{C}^\beta}, \Delta\delta_{\text{C}'}, \Delta\delta_{\text{H}^\alpha})$, $\Delta\delta_{\text{C}^\alpha} - \Delta\delta_{\text{C}^\beta}$ polarization differences, and `BLOSUM62` log-odds substitution penalties over both the local 3-mer (`[i-1, i, i+1]`, position weights `[1.15, 2.50, 1.15]`) and flanking 9-mer (`[-4..-2, +2..+4]`, context weights `[0.4, 0.6, 1.0, 1.0, 0.6, 0.4]`, outer scale `1.15`) windows.
3. **Circular cluster consensus**: From the top $K = 10$ fragment matches, residue $i$ is classified as:
   - `Good`: $S^2_{\text{RCI}} \ge 0.65$, dominant $40^\circ$-radius $(\phi, \psi)$ cluster contains $\ge 7/10$ (`70%`) matches, combined Boltzmann-weighted circular standard deviation $\sqrt{\sigma_\phi^2 + \sigma_\psi^2} \le 25^\circ$, and circular mean $(\hat{\phi}_i, \hat{\psi}_i)$ lies strictly inside Lovell allowed Ramachandran basins (so `0` predicted Ramachandran outliers holds by construction on `Good` calls; accuracy is evaluated via `phi_mae_deg`, `psi_mae_deg`, combined circular MAE, and non-tautological `wrong_basin_vs_truth`). When $\ge 7$ cluster members agree within $36^\circ$ on $\chi_1$ (`non-Gly/Ala`), $\hat{\chi}_1 \pm \sigma_{\chi_1}$ is computed internally in `TorsionPredictionReport`, while downstream TSV/NEF/JSON and `DihedralRestraint` emission gate `CHI1` behind opt-in `TorsionExportOptions { include_chi1: false }` (`--include-chi1` on `nmrassign torsions` and `nmrassign structure --torsion-db`, omitted by default because 9-protein in-sample `chi1_mae_deg = 33.63°` across `n = 81` residues).
   - `Dynamic`: $S^2_{\text{RCI}} < 0.65$ (flexible loop / terminus / IDP).
   - `Ambiguous`: $S^2_{\text{RCI}} \ge 0.65$ with multimodal or wide fragment spread (abstains cleanly rather than emitting a wrong basin restraint).
   - `Unassigned`: chain terminus (`i = 0` or `i = L - 1`) or fewer than 2 observed shifts at residue $i$.

## 3. 4-Layer `HomologFirewall` + Audit Digest & Identity Stratification (`Q5`)

Every benchmark evaluation (`predict_torsions_with_firewall`) enforces a deterministic 4-layer non-homologous exclusion firewall (`HomologFirewallQuery`) between the query protein and `TorsionDb`, paired with a cryptographic exclusion-manifest audit digest and sequence-identity stratification:

1. **Layer 1 — Protein / `UniProt` Family Exclusion**: Excludes every template matching any query `PDB` ID, `BMRB` ID, `UniProt` primary accession, or protein family / superfamily ID (eliminating point mutants, orthologs, and complexes).
2. **Layer 2 — Fold Topology Exclusion**: Excludes every template sharing the same `ECOD` H-group or `CATH` `C.A.T` topology fold group (`topology_id`, e.g., `3.10.20` $\beta$-grasp ubiquitin-like fold).
3. **Layer 3 — Configurable Global, Local & K-mer Sequence Exclusion (`max_seq_identity`, default `0.25`)**: Excludes any template with sequence identity `> max_seq_identity` (`HomologFirewallConfig { max_seq_identity: 0.25 }` by default; configurable via `HomologFirewall::with_max_seq_identity` / `predict_torsions_with_firewall_and_cutoff`) over an aligned span covering $\ge 50\%$ of **either** the query length **or** the template length under **both** shorter-spanning Needleman–Wunsch (end-to-end across $\min(L_{\text{query}}, L_{\text{template}})$, preventing corner-overhang escapes) and local Smith–Waterman alignment — evaluated under **both** `BLOSUM62` (`-11/-1`) and exact-identity (`match +3, mismatch -1, gap -11/-2`) scoring so non-conservative homologs with negative `BLOSUM62` scores never escape — **plus** any template sharing a 7-mer window with $\le 1$ mismatch ($\ge 6/7$ identical residues).
4. **Layer 4 — 3D Structural `TM-score` Exclusion**: Whenever query reference $\text{C}^\alpha$ coordinates exist in benchmark evaluation, computes exact clean-room Zhang–Skolnick `TM-score` (`tm_score_ca` with multi-seed fragment/sliding-window superposition and iterative $d_0(L)$ refinement, normalized by $\min(L_{\text{query}}, L_{\text{template}})$) and excludes any template with $\text{TM-score} \ge 0.50$.

## 4. Residual Dipolar Couplings (`RDC`), Saupe SVD & WebGPU Refinement (`rdc.rs`, `rdc_gpu.rs`)

### 4.1 Multi-Medium Dipolar Coupling Physics
For internuclear unit vector $\hat{\mathbf{u}} = (\mathbf{r}_B - \mathbf{r}_A) / \|\mathbf{r}_B - \mathbf{r}_A\|$ in alignment medium $m$, the secular dipolar coupling is linear in the traceless symmetric $3 \times 3$ Saupe order matrix $\mathbf{S}^{(m)}$ ($\text{Tr}(\mathbf{S}^{(m)}) = 0$, $\mathbf{S}^{(m)} = (\mathbf{S}^{(m)})^\top$):

$$D_{\text{calc}}(\hat{\mathbf{u}}; \mathbf{S}^{(m)}) = D_{\max}^{AB}\,\hat{\mathbf{u}}^\top \mathbf{S}^{(m)} \hat{\mathbf{u}}$$

Supported bond types (`RdcBondType`), covalent geometry vs libration-adjusted dipolar prefactors $D_{\max}^{AB}$ (Ottiger & Bax, *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* **1998**, 120, 12334–12341; overrideable per row via explicit `d_max_hz`), and sign conventions (`RdcSignConvention`):
- `1D_NH` (`N`–`H`, covalent `NeRF` $r_{\text{N–H}} = 1.010\text{ \AA}$, libration-averaged $r_{\text{eff}} = 1.040\text{ \AA}$, sign-normalized $D_{\max}^{\text{NH}} = +21\,655.82\text{ Hz}$ or physical-$\gamma$ $-21\,655.82\text{ Hz}$).
- `1D_CAHA` (`CA`–`HA` on chiral L-residues, or explicit tetrahedral prochiral `CA`–`HA2` [`pro-R`] and `CA`–`HA3` [`pro-S`] on `GLY` at static Engh & Huber covalent `NeRF` bond length `R_CAHA_GEOM_A = 1.090 Å` = `BOND_CA_HA`, $\approx 109.5^\circ$ apart — rejecting `HA2`/`HA3` on non-`GLY` and ambiguous bare `HA` on `GLY`; because $\hat{\mathbf{u}}$ is a unit vector, covalent `1.090 Å` controls only directional placement while fast $\text{C}^\alpha\text{–H}^\alpha$ bond-bending libration scales the dipolar prefactor via `R_CAHA_EFF_A = 1.105 Å`, $D_{\max}^{\text{CAHA}} = +44\,782.43\text{ Hz}$, ratio `2.068` to $D_{\max}^{\text{NH}}$).
- `1D_CACO` (`CA`–`C`, $r_{\text{eff}} = 1.525\text{ \AA}$, $D_{\max}^{\text{CACO}} = +4\,284.75\text{ Hz}$).
- `1D_NC'` (`C_{i-1}`–`N_i`, $r_{\text{eff}} = 1.329\text{ \AA}$, sign-normalized $D_{\max}^{\text{NC}'} = +2\,610.02\text{ Hz}$ or physical-$\gamma$ $-2\,610.02\text{ Hz}$).
- `2D_HNC'` / `1D_HNC'` (`RdcBondType::HNCPrime`, `iupac_coupling_label() == "2D_HNC'"`: **two-bond** through-space dipolar coupling $^2D(\text{H}^{\text{N}}_i, \text{C}'_{i-1})$ across the rigid trans peptide plane at $r_{\text{eff}} = 2.100\text{ \AA}$, $D_{\max}^{\text{HNC}'} = +6\,525.10\text{ Hz}$ since $\gamma_{^1\text{H}} > 0$ and $\gamma_{^{13}\text{C}} > 0$; both strict IUPAC `2D_HNC'` and legacy CLI/NEF `1D_HNC'` TSV tokens are accepted).
- **Explicit Sign Conventions (`RdcSignConvention`)**: Because $\gamma_{^{15}\text{N}} < 0$ whereas $\gamma_{^1\text{H}}, \gamma_{^{13}\text{C}} > 0$, `RdcSignConvention::SignNormalized` (default, matching `wwPDB` `1D3Z`/`3GB1` restraint tables where $^{15}\text{N}$ signs are absorbed into $D_{\text{obs}}$ so parallel $\text{N–H}$ and $\text{C}^\alpha\text{–H}^\alpha$ vectors share the same sign of $D_{\text{obs}} / D_{\max}$) uses $D_{\max}^{AB} > 0$, whereas `RdcSignConvention::PhysicalGammaSign` (`# sign_convention: physical_gamma` TSV directive or `RdcBondType::signed_d_max_hz`) assigns $D_{\max}$ with `physical_gamma_sign()` (`-21,655.82 Hz` for `1D_NH` and `-2,610.02 Hz` for `1D_NC'`, positive for `1D_CAHA`, `1D_CACO`, and `2D_HNC'`). Converting via `normalize_rdc_sign_convention` leaves the fitted Saupe tensor $\mathbf{S}^{(m)}$, $D_a$, $R$, Cornilescu $Q$, and analytical forces invariant to `< 1e-12`.

### 4.2 Exact 5-Parameter Losonczi Saupe Tensor SVD & Safety Invariants
Substituting $S_{xx} = -(S_{yy} + S_{zz})$ expresses $D_{\text{calc}}$ linearly in the 5 independent components $\mathbf{s} = (S_{yy}, S_{zz}, S_{xy}, S_{xz}, S_{yz})^\top$:

$$\frac{D_{\text{calc},k}}{\sigma_k} = \frac{D_{\max,k}}{\sigma_k}\Big[(u_y^2 - u_x^2)S_{yy} + (u_z^2 - u_x^2)S_{zz} + 2u_x u_y S_{xy} + 2u_x u_z S_{xz} + 2u_y u_z S_{yz}\Big]$$

`fit_saupe_tensor_svd` solves $\min_{\mathbf{s}} \|\mathbf{A}\mathbf{s} - \mathbf{b}\|_2^2$ over working (`!is_free`) couplings via one-sided Hestenes–Jacobi thin SVD (`m x 5`, without forming $\mathbf{A}^\top\mathbf{A}$), enforcing strict numerical safety invariants:
1. Requires $n_{\text{work}} \ge 6$ finite RDCs per medium with $\sigma_k > 0$ and finite $D_{\text{obs},k}, D_{\max,k}$.
2. Rejects collinear or coplanar bond vector geometries whenever $\kappa(\mathbf{A}) = \sigma_{\max} / \sigma_{\min} > 10^8$ (`Err`).
3. Diagonalizes $\mathbf{S}^{(m)} = \mathbf{R}\,\text{diag}(S_{xx}^{\text{PAS}}, S_{yy}^{\text{PAS}}, S_{zz}^{\text{PAS}})\,\mathbf{R}^\top$ in canonical principal-axis order $|S_{zz}^{\text{PAS}}| \ge |S_{yy}^{\text{PAS}}| \ge |S_{xx}^{\text{PAS}}|$, guaranteeing $D_a^{\text{NH}} = \frac{1}{2} D_{\max}^{\text{NH}} S_{zz}^{\text{PAS}}$, $0 \le R = \frac{S_{xx}^{\text{PAS}} - S_{yy}^{\text{PAS}}}{S_{zz}^{\text{PAS}}} \le \frac{2}{3}$, and canonical right-handed `SO(3)` `ZYZ` Euler angles $(\alpha, \beta, \gamma)$.
4. Computes normalized Cornilescu $Q$ (`q_work`) and held-out cross-validated $Q_{\text{free}}$ (`q_free`):

$$Q = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_k (\hat{D}_{\text{obs},k} - \hat{D}_{\text{calc},k})^2}{\sum_k \hat{D}_{\text{obs},k}^2}}, \qquad \hat{D}_k = D_k \cdot \frac{D_{\max}^{\text{NH}}}{D_{\max,k}}$$

returning `f64::NAN` whenever $\sum_k \hat{D}_{\text{obs},k}^2 \le 10^{-15}$ (and treating `NaN` as strictly worst in `rdc_q_is_better`).

### 4.3 Variable-Projection Torsion Refinement & Batched WebGPU Kernel
Under variable projection $\tilde{E}_{\text{RDC}}(\boldsymbol{\phi}, \boldsymbol{\psi}) = \min_{\{\mathbf{S}^{(m)}\}} \frac{1}{2} w_{\text{RDC}} \sum_{m, k \in \mathcal{W}_m} \left(\frac{D_{\text{calc},k}(\mathbf{S}^{(m)}) - D_{\text{obs},k}}{\sigma_k}\right)^2$, the Envelope Theorem ($\partial E / \partial \mathbf{S}^{(m)}\big|_{\mathbf{S}^{*(m)}} = \mathbf{0}$) implies exact Cartesian forces at optimal $\mathbf{S}^{*(m)}$:

$$\frac{\partial \tilde{E}_{\text{RDC}}}{\partial \mathbf{r}_{B,k}} = w_{\text{RDC}} \frac{D_{\text{calc},k} - D_{\text{obs},k}}{\sigma_k^2} \cdot \frac{2 D_{\max,k}}{\|\mathbf{v}_k\|} \left(\mathbf{S}^{*(m)}\hat{\mathbf{u}}_k - (\hat{\mathbf{u}}_k^\top \mathbf{S}^{*(m)} \hat{\mathbf{u}}_k)\hat{\mathbf{u}}_k\right), \qquad \frac{\partial \tilde{E}_{\text{RDC}}}{\partial \mathbf{r}_{A,k}} = -\frac{\partial \tilde{E}_{\text{RDC}}}{\partial \mathbf{r}_{B,k}}$$

- **CPU (`structure::refine_ensemble_with_rdcs`)**: Multi-threaded staged refinement combining full-circle compensating peptide-plane crankshaft proposals (including exact $180^\circ$ peptide-plane inversions and canonical Ramachandran turn-basin sweeps), soft-core Langevin annealing, bounded $C^1$ log-Lorentzian flat-bottom dihedral tails (preventing rare database basin-mirror outliers from overriding multi-bond RDCs), and two-loop `L-BFGS` in $(\phi, \psi, \omega)$ torsion space with harmonic Engh–Huber / Cornilescu (*JACS* **1998**) peptide-planarity restraints ($\sigma_\omega \approx 3.7^\circ$, clamped within $\pm 7.5^\circ$ of trans/cis equilibrium) over `!is_free` couplings only. In multi-cycle ADR structure calculation (`calculate_structure_with_rdcs_and_dihedrals`), initial NOESY candidate lists are re-weighted non-destructively against RDC-refined coordinates at every cycle so contacts prematurely pruned at coarse Cycle-0 resolution are recovered.
- **WebGPU (`nmr_gpu::rdc_gpu` + `shaders/rdc_refine.wgsl`)**: Evaluates `NeRF` backbone + chiral $\text{H}^\alpha/\text{C}^\beta$ + explicit tetrahedral `GLY` `HA2`/`HA3` forward kinematics, multi-medium harmonic RDC residuals, rigid-segment forces/torques, and $O(L)$ backward suffix-sum $(\partial E / \partial \phi_i, \partial E / \partial \psi_i)$ gradients across a multi-conformer ensemble, followed by `f64` host L-BFGS polish (`max_rel_energy_err = 1.252e-6`, `max_rel_grad_err = 6.038e-6 < 1e-5` across 16 conformers $\times$ 2 media $\times$ 5 bond types; `max_rel_e = 1.551e-6`, `max_rel_g = 1.687e-5` across `310` conformers with signed `d_max_hz` and `ByPeptidePlane` holdout, compiling cleanly on both native and `wasm32-unknown-unknown` with `#[cfg(not(target_arch = "wasm32"))]` sync wrappers over `read_buffer_vec_async`). Before any GPU allocation, `validate_rdc_gpu_dispatch_limits` validates all 8 uniform and storage buffers (`params_buf`, `flags_buf`, `torsions_buf`, `saupe_buf`, `terms_buf`, `scratch_buf`, `energies_buf`, `grads_buf`) against `max_buffer_size`, `max_storage_buffer_binding_size`, `max_uniform_buffer_binding_size`, and `max_compute_workgroups_per_dimension` (returning `Err` cleanly without panicking), and large ensembles are chunked into batches of `MAX_RDC_CONFORMERS_PER_DISPATCH = 256` conformers per `queue.submit` to prevent OS GPU driver TDR watchdog timeouts.
- **Peptide-Plane-Stratified `Q_free` Holdout (`RdcFreeStratification::ByPeptidePlane`, `nef.rs`, `report.rs`, `nmr-cli`)**: Because `1D_NH(i)`, `1D_NC'(i-1, i)`, `2D_HNC'(i-1, i)`, and `1D_CACO(i-1)` all lie in the same rigid peptide plane `(i-1, i)` (and couple tightly to `1D_CAHA(i)` via $(\phi_i, \psi_{i-1})$), holding out individual couplings independently leaks plane orientation from sibling working couplings into $Q_{\text{free}}$ (Clore & Garrett, *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* **1999**, 121, 9008–9012). `partition_rdc_free_set` therefore defaults to `RdcFreeStratification::ByPeptidePlane` (`rdc_peptide_plane_id`), assigning every coupling on structural unit `plane_id = i` synchronously across all alignment media to either working or `is_free` (`0` mixed work/free planes), while `partition_rdc_free_set_with_mode(..., RdcFreeStratification::ByCoupling)` retains per-coupling stratification for explicit comparison. `nmrassign structure --rdc` holds out `20%` of peptide planes by default (`--rdc-free-frac 0.20`; `--rdc-free-frac 0.0` disables holdout), and both `structure --rdc` and `report --pdb --rdc` call `count_unresolved_rdc_restraints` to emit an explicit `stderr` warning whenever RDC rows are dropped due to unresolved residues or coordinates. Full `NEF v1.1` `nef_rdc_restraint_list` and `PHI`/`PSI` (`--include-chi1` for `CHI1`) `nef_dihedral_restraint_list` round-trip and XML-escaped `D_calc` vs `D_obs` SVG scatter reports are supported.
- **Self-Consistency & Independent Non-Circular X-Ray Crystal Validation (`P1-9`)**:
  1. *Optimizer & CPU/WebGPU Parity (`1D3Z` self-consistency, `ByPeptidePlane` `20%` holdout)*: Using forward-simulated `1D3Z` NOEs + `1D3Z` 2-medium bicelle RDCs under `ByPeptidePlane` `20%` holdout (`free_seed = 199807`), CPU refinement improves `Q_work: 0.9159 -> 0.1078`, `ByPeptidePlane Q_free: 1.0210 -> 0.2846`, and Ordered BB RMSD (`1..71`) `3.077 Å -> 0.624 Å` (`Rama favored: 96.8% -> 99.5%`). On matched-input `free_seed = 4242` (`6` starting conformers at `4.929 Å`, `Q_work = 0.6335`, `plane Q_free = 0.6107`), CPU `f64` reaches `Q_work = 0.1121, plane Q_free = 0.2846, BB RMSD = 0.739 Å` vs WebGPU + `f64` polish `Q_work = 0.1099, plane Q_free = 0.3077, BB RMSD = 0.733 Å`.
  2. *Non-Circular Benchmark A — Human Ubiquitin (`1D3Z` experimental NOEs $\to$ `1D3Z` bicelle RDCs $\to$ independent 1987 `1.8 Å` X-ray `1UBQ`, Vijay-Kumar et al. J. Mol. Biol. 1987)*: Folded de novo from extended coordinates using **only** `2,727` public experimental NOE distance restraints from `wwPDB` `1D3Z` (`data/fixtures/ubiquitin_1d3z_exp_noe.tsv`, zero simulated distances, zero chemical shifts) and refined against `1D3Z` 2-medium bicelle RDCs (`ByPeptidePlane` `20%` holdout) before evaluating against the independent 1987 `1.8 Å` crystal structure `1UBQ` (`data/fixtures/ubiquitin_1ubq_xray.pdb`, direct unrefined crystal fit `Q_work = 0.2042, plane Q_free = 0.2437`). Experimental NOE-only folding gives `Q_work = 0.8911, plane Q_free = 0.8708`, and `1UBQ` (`1..71`) BB RMSD `lowE = 3.502 Å` (`mean = 4.040 Å`); adding working bicelle RDCs improves `Q_work -> 0.1184`, held-out `ByPeptidePlane Q_free -> 0.3510`, and independent `1UBQ` (`1..71`) BB RMSD to **`lowE = 0.792 Å` (`mean = 1.570 Å`)**.
  3. *Non-Circular Benchmark B — Streptococcal GB1 (`3GB1` experimental NOEs + `BMRB 7280` firewalled shifts $\to$ `3GB1` TMV & bicelle RDCs $\to$ independent 1994 `2.07 Å` X-ray `1PGA`, Gallagher et al. Biochemistry 1994)*: Folded de novo from extended coordinates using `671` public experimental NOE restraints (`data/fixtures/gb1_3gb1_exp_noe.tsv`) plus `BMRB 7280` solution chemical shifts (`data/fixtures/gb1_bmr7280_shifts.str`, Wilton et al. *J. Mol. Biol.* **2008**) passed strictly through the 4-layer `HomologFirewall` (`max_retained_seq_identity <= 25%`, excluding all `GB1` / `P06654` / `CATH 3.10.20` $\beta$-grasp templates, `query_ca_coords: None` so zero `1PGA`/`3GB1` coordinates ever enter folding) and refined against `3GB1` 2-medium `TMV` (`Da = +6.76 Hz, R = 0.620`) + `bicelle` (`Da = -10.01 Hz, R = 0.248`) multi-bond (`1D_NH`, `1D_NC'`, `2D_HNC'`) RDCs (`data/fixtures/gb1_3gb1_rdc.tsv`, `ByPeptidePlane` `20%` holdout) before evaluating against the independent 1994 `2.07 Å` crystal structure `1PGA` (`data/pdb_refs/1pga.pdb`, direct unrefined crystal fit `1D_NH Q = 0.1636, multi-bond Q_work = 0.3327, plane Q_free = 0.3409`). Pre-RDC experimental NOE + firewalled-shift folding gives `Q_work = 0.8727, plane Q_free = 1.0065`, and `1PGA` (`1..56`) BB RMSD `lowE = 2.320 Å` (`mean = 3.237 Å`); adding working TMV + bicelle RDCs improves `Q_work -> 0.1566`, held-out `ByPeptidePlane Q_free -> 0.5075` multi-bond (`1D_NH Q_work = 0.0596`, held-out **`1D_NH plane Q_free = 0.2809`**), and independent `1PGA` (`1..56`) BB RMSD to **`lowE = 1.033 Å` (`mean = 1.483 Å`)**.
