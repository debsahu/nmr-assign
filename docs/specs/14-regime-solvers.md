## 1. Protonless `13C`-Detected IDP Experiments, Narrow-Dispersion Picker & Volumetric Simulator (`T11.1`, `crates/nmr-core/src/c13_detected.rs`)

### 1.1 Physical Motivation: Amide Exchange Broadening vs Direct `13C'` Detection
Standard globular triple-resonance experiments (`1H–15N HSQC`, `HNCA`, `HNCACB`, `HNCO`; see `03-assignment.md`) detect amide protons $^1\text{H}^{\text{N}}$ and fail in two hallmark IDP regimes:
1. **Base-catalyzed solvent exchange at physiological pH/temperature**: Under the Bai–Englander random-coil exchange model (`intrinsic_amide_h_exchange_rate`; Bai et al., 1993), exposed disordered amides at $\text{pH } 7.4,\ T = 310\text{ K}$ experience exchange rates $k_{\text{ex}} = k_{\text{A}} 10^{-\text{pH}} + k_{\text{B}} 10^{\text{pH}-\text{p}K_w(T)} + k_{\text{W}} \sim 20\text{–}150\text{ s}^{-1}$ ($\Delta H^\ddagger \approx 71.1\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}$, $k_{\text{B,ref}}(\text{pH } 7, 293.15\text{ K}) \approx 8.5\text{ s}^{-1}$). The resulting transverse linewidth broadening and refocused-INEPT ($2\tau \approx 10.8\text{ ms}$) coherence loss
   $$\text{FWHM}_{\text{eff}}(^1\text{H}^{\text{N}}) = \text{FWHM}_0 + \frac{k_{\text{ex}}}{\pi}, \qquad A_{\text{HSQC}} = A_0\,\frac{\text{FWHM}_0}{\text{FWHM}_{\text{eff}}}\,\exp(-2\tau k_{\text{ex}})$$
   bleaches most non-Proline cross-peaks below the detection threshold (`simulate_h1_exchange_vs_c13_con` $\to$ `H1ExchangeComparison`), whereas non-exchangeable $^1\text{H}^\alpha$-start or $^{13}\text{C}$-start direct $^{13}\text{C}'$ detection remains immune to amide exchange.
2. **Proline abundance and narrow `1H` dispersion**: Disordered transactivation and signaling domains routinely contain `15–25%` Proline (`-Xaa-Pro-` and polyproline `-Pro-Pro-` tracts), which lack $^1\text{H}^{\text{N}}$ altogether and fracture `1H`-detected sequential walks into isolated short islands. Direct $^{13}\text{C}'$ detection anchored on `2D CON` observes every peptide bond $k \in \{1, \dots, L-1\}$ continuously, shifting `Pro` imino $^{15}\text{N}$ into a clean downfield window (`132–142 ppm` vs `106–129 ppm` for secondary amides; `is_proline_imino_region`, threshold `>= 131.5 ppm`).

### 1.3 Narrow-Dispersion Deconvolving Picker & IPAP Ghost Suppression (`C13PickerConfig`)
In IDPs, non-`Gly`/`Pro` carbonyl shifts compress into `< 2.5 ppm` (`~173.5–176.5 ppm`) and `CA` shifts within a residue type cluster within `< 0.4 ppm`, while imperfect `IPAP` / `S3E` in-phase/antiphase sum/difference virtual decoupling leaves residual uncancelled doublet satellites along `F_direct(13C')` at full $\pm {}^{1}J_{\text{C}'\text{CA}}$ (`~ ±53.5 Hz`, i.e. `±0.304 ppm` at `700 MHz` $^{1}\text{H}$ / `176.0 MHz` $^{13}\text{C}$ and `±0.266 ppm` at `800 MHz` $^{1}\text{H}$ / `201.2 MHz` $^{13}\text{C}$ — since IPAP adds/subtracts the $\pm \frac{1}{2}{}^{1}J_{\text{C}'\text{CA}}$ doublet components after shifting both by $+\frac{1}{2}{}^{1}J_{\text{C}'\text{CA}}$ and $-\frac{1}{2}{}^{1}J_{\text{C}'\text{CA}}$, placing the cancelled partner at $\pm {}^{1}J_{\text{C}'\text{CA}}$ from the reconstructed singlet; Bermel et al., 2006). Every tolerance in `C13PickerConfig` is parameterized in physical **Hz** and dynamically converted via $\Delta\delta_{\text{ppm}}(B_0) = \Delta\nu_{\text{Hz}} / \nu_{\text{obs,MHz}}(B_0)$ across `500–950 MHz`:

1. **`pick_con_2d` $\to$ `Vec<Con2DPeak>`**:
   - Estimates thermal noise $\hat{\sigma}_{\text{MAD}}$ via two-pass outlier-trimmed median absolute deviation, extracts `3x3` local maxima above `snr_2d = 5.0` with sub-voxel parabolic refinement, and runs connected-component residual Gaussian shoulder deconvolution (`shoulder_snr_2d = 5.2`, `shoulder_min_rel_amp = 0.14`, `max_cluster_components = 16`, minimum separation `min_sep_c_prime_hz = 3.0 Hz`, `min_sep_n15_hz = 3.5 Hz`) to split blended IDP dipeptide clusters.
   - **Symmetric IPAP `1J(C',CA)` Satellite Filter**: Rejects candidate same-sign satellite peaks at $|\Delta\nu_{\text{C}'}| \in [45.0, 62.0]\text{ Hz}$ (`ipap_j_window_hz`) and $|\Delta\nu_{\text{N}}| \le 4.0\text{ Hz}$ around stronger parent `(C', N)` roots whenever candidate amplitude lies within the multi-parent residual + `2.5 σ_noise` budget (`ipap_max_residual_frac = 0.105`, capped at `0.24` of the strongest parent), or when a symmetric $\pm {}^{1}J_{\text{C}'\text{CA}}$ doublet pair flanks the parent (`<= 0.16` amplitude ratio), without suppressing genuine overlapping IDP roots.
2. **`pick_caco_2d` $\to$ `Vec<Caco2DPeak>`**:
   - Signed positive/negative extrema picker (`CacoPeakKind::{GlyOppositePhase, GlyRegion, SerThrProDownfield, GeneralAliphatic}`), preserving inverted-phase `Gly` `(C', CA ~ 45.1 ppm, sign = -1)` peaks alongside positive non-`Gly` cross-peaks and applying full-`1J(C',CA)` IPAP ghost suppression along `C'`.
3. **`pick_con_anchored_3d` / `pick_con_anchored_3d_with_diagnostics` $\to$ `(Vec<C13Strip3DPeak>, C13PickerDriftDiagnostic)`**:
   - Auto-registers systematic `3D` vs `2D CON` `(ΔC', ΔN)` carrier offsets (`auto_register_3d_cubes = true`, clamped to `±0.40 ppm C'` and `±0.60 ppm 15N`), emitting an explicit `C13PickerDriftDiagnostic` clamp warning whenever `|ΔC'| >= 0.38 ppm` or `|ΔN| >= 0.57 ppm`; for `3D NCACO`, 1D axis registration (`fit_ncaco_1d_offset`) adopts a non-zero offset only when its Gaussian cross-correlation strictly beats `d = 0.0` by `> 1.5%` (`|best_d| >= 0.008 ppm`). Extracts bilinearly interpolated 1D indirect `z`-strips at each `CON` root (`strip_tol_c_prime_hz = 5.5 Hz`, `strip_tol_n15_hz = 6.5 Hz`) for `CANCO`, `CBCACON`, and `CCC(CO)N` (`snr_3d = 4.5`, `min_sep_c_aliph_hz = 4.2 Hz`), and performs full 3D local-extrema picking on `NCACO` (linking `anchor_root_idx` when `(C'_k, N_k)` happens to coincide with an existing `CON` root and retaining `anchor_root_idx = None` otherwise so all intraresidue `(C'_k, N_k, CA_k)` peaks are preserved).

## 2. Continuous Through-Proline `13C`-Detected IDP Backbone Solver (`T11.2`, `P1-7`/`Q3` & `P1-11`, `crates/nmr-core/src/idp_solver.rs`, `crates/nmr-gpu/src/idp_gpu.rs`)

### 2.1 `CON(k)` Dipeptide Spin-System Formulation (`ConDipeptideSystem`)
Unlike `1H`-detected `SpinSystem`s (which skip `Pro` slots with `FORBID`), each `13C`-detected `ConDipeptideSystem` $s \in \{0, \dots, S-1\}$ represents a **peptide bond** $(k-1, k)$ anchored at $(C'_{k-1}, N_k)$ and can map to **every sequence slot $k \in \{1, \dots, L-1\}$ continuously across `-Xaa-Pro-` and `-Pro-Pro-` boundaries** (only N-terminal $k = 0$ is `FORBID = -1.0e9` because residue `0` has no preceding peptide carbonyl $C'_{-1}$):
- Anchor shifts: `c_prev` ($C'_{k-1}$), `n_curr` ($N_k$).
- Bounded candidate arrays (`MAX_IDP_CANDS = 6`, `MAX_IDP_CB_CANDS = 4`, `MAX_IDP_SC_CANDS = 6`; matrix allocation bounded at `MAX_IDP_MATRIX_CELLS = 25_000_000`, returning `Err` on `S * K` or `S * S` overflow in `try_idp_unary_matrix`, `try_idp_pair_matrix`, `try_assign_idp_13c_cpu`, `try_assign_idp_13c_from_picked`, and `IdpGpuSolver`): `ca_prev` ($CA_{k-1}$ from `CBCACON`/`CANCO`), `cb_prev` (up to `4` $CB_{k-1}$ candidates from `CBCACON`/`CCC(CO)N`), `ca_curr` ($CA_k$ from `CANCO` after subtracting `CBCACON`-tagged $CA_{k-1}$, plus joint `(N, CA_i)`-gated `NCACO` corroboration), `c_curr` ($C'_k$ from `NCACO` + `2D CACO`), `sidechain_c_prev` ($C^{\text{ali}}_{k-1}$ from `CCC(CO)N`), `prev_gly_opposite_phase` (from inverted `2D CACO`), `has_cbcacon_evidence`, and `registration_warnings`.

### 2.2 Sequence-Specific `ncIDP` Random-Coil Predictor & Look-Elsewhere-Guarded Unsupervised Registration
1. **`predict_ncidp_shifts(sequence, cfg)`**:
   Evaluates sequence-specific disordered random-coil chemical shifts `(N, C', CA, CB)` across the primary sequence by combining:
   - Tamiola, Acar & Mulder (2010) `ncIDP` base random-coil shifts at `298.15 K, pH 6.5` (including imino `Pro 15N = 136.8 ppm`),
   - Zero-centered preceding-residue $(i-1)$ neighbor corrections (with explicit tertiary-imide `Xaa-Pro` $\Delta\delta_N$ modulation) and following-residue $(i+1)$ neighbor corrections (capturing the hallmark pre-Proline shifts $\Delta\delta_{\text{CA}} = -1.95\text{ ppm}$, $\Delta\delta_{\text{C}'} = -1.95\text{ ppm}$, $\Delta\delta_{\text{N}} = +1.65\text{ ppm}$, $\Delta\delta_{\text{CB}} = -0.35\text{ ppm}$ when $\text{seq}[k+1] = \text{PRO}$),
   - Platzer, Okon & McIntosh (2014) Henderson–Hasselbalch pH titration shifts for ionizable `Asp`, `Glu`, `His`, `Cys`, `Tyr`, `Lys`, and termini, and
   - Kjaergaard, Brander & Poulsen (2011) linear amide/aliphatic temperature coefficients $\frac{\partial\delta}{\partial T}(T - 298.15\text{ K})$.
2. **Unlabelled Permutation-Invariant Reference Offset Estimation (`estimate_idp_reference_offsets` / `fit_1d_unlabelled_marginal_offset`, `P1-11`)**:
   Estimates systematic referencing offsets `IdpShiftOffsets { d_n, d_c, d_ca, d_cb }` strictly from the canonically sorted unordered multisets of observed shifts (including up to `MAX_IDP_CB_CANDS = 4` `CB` candidates per system) vs sequence-predicted `ncIDP` marginal mixtures via grid log-likelihood maximization + 4-step posterior-responsibility Huber IRLS (`0.0` permutation sensitivity; warning if any global axis hits `>= 3.80 ppm` near the `±4.00 ppm` bound). To control look-elsewhere false positives over the `±4.00 ppm` search interval under composite marginal correlation, a non-zero offset $\hat{d}$ ($|\hat{d}| \ge 0.015\text{ ppm}$) is accepted if and only if its composite log-likelihood improvement over $d = 0$ exceeds the Godambe-scaled Wilks + look-elsewhere critical threshold (Wilks *Ann. Math. Stat.* 1938; Varin, Reid & Firth *Stat. Sinica* 2011; Gross & Vitells *Eur. Phys. J. C* 2010):
   $$\ell(\hat{d}) - \ell(0) > \lambda_{\text{Godambe}}\left[\ln N_{\text{grid,eff}} + \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_{1,0.95}\right], \qquad N_{\text{grid,eff}} = \max\!\left(8,\; \left\lceil\frac{2\,\Delta_{\max}}{\sqrt{2}\,s_{\text{harm}}}\right\rceil\right), \quad \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_{1,0.95} = 1.92073\text{ nats}$$
   where $\lambda_{\text{Godambe}} = \text{clamp}(s_{\text{harm}}^2 \bar{h},\, 0.12,\, 1.0)$ is the composite-to-labelled curvature ratio at $d = 0$.
3. **Unsupervised Inter-Experiment Registration & Joint `(N, CA_i)` `NCACO` Gating (`register_and_build_con_dipeptide_systems` / `fit_unlabelled_pairwise_drift_grouped`, `P1-11`)**:
   Aligns indirect `CA` axes of `CBCACON`/`CCC(CO)N` onto `CANCO`, aligns `2D CACO` `(C', CA)` onto `(CON, CANCO)` via two-pass `CA`-gated carbonyl pairing (`|ΔCA| <= 1.65 ppm` pass 1, `|ΔCA - d_CA| <= 0.22 ppm` pass 2 after fitting `d_CA`, suppressing $O(N^2)$ random-coil carbonyl background pairs), and registers both anchored and unanchored `3D NCACO` `(ΔC', ΔN, ΔCA)` carrier drifts unsupervised (`±0.70 ppm` clamp, probing out to `±1.10 ppm` to emit explicit `registration_warnings` whenever `|raw_drift| >= 0.65 ppm`). Every pairwise registration axis fits a 2-component Gaussian-coincidence vs uniform-background EM profile log-likelihood and adopts a non-zero drift (`|best_d| >= 0.012 ppm`) only when (i) $\ell_{\text{prof}}(\hat{d}) - \ell_{\text{prof}}(0) > \ln N_{\text{grid,eff}} + \frac{1}{2}\chi^2_{1,0.95}$ ($N_{\text{grid,eff}} = \max(12, \lceil 2\,\Delta_{\text{probe}}/(\sqrt{2}\,\text{bw})\rceil)$) and (ii) at least `>= 5` distinct query roots/peaks contribute differences within `±2.5 bw` of $\hat{d}$ (preventing sparse unanchored noise spikes from acquiring $O(M)$ Cartesian-product votes). Verified by an $N = 250$ zero-offset null-hypothesis Monte Carlo audit (`0.80% < 5.0%` family-wise false non-zero rate across `(dN, dC', dCA, dCB)`, `0/250` false carrier-drift clamp warnings, and `20/20 = 100%` recovery of genuine `+2.0 ppm 13C` / `+1.5 ppm 15N` `DSS`/`TSP` offsets). Unanchored and shared-`15N` `NCACO` peaks require **joint `(N_k, CA_k)` compatibility** with `CANCO` (`|ΔN| <= 0.145 ppm` / `0.16 ppm` Pro and `|ΔCA| <= 0.24 ppm`, with rescue restricted to roots lacking a 2-peak `CANCO` pair) and downweight effective amplitude by `fanout^{-0.65}` so degenerate `15N` slices never broadcast unpenalized across unrelated `CON` roots.

### 2.3 Dipeptide Unary $U[s, k]$ & Multiplicity-Penalized Sequential Pair $P[s_1, s_2]$ Scoring
1. **Dipeptide Unary Score $U[s, k]$ (`idp_unary_matrix` / WGSL `compute_idp_unary`)**:
   For peptide-bond system $s$ placed at sequence dipeptide $(k-1, k)$ ($k \ge 1$):
   $$U[s, k] = b_{\text{sys}}(s) + \sum_{\nu \in \{N_k, C'_{k-1}, CA_k, CA_{k-1}, CB_{k-1}, C'_k\}} \ln\frac{p_{\text{mix}}(\delta_\nu \mid \mu_{\nu,k}, \sigma_\nu)}{p_{\text{bg}}(\delta_\nu)} + \text{LLR}_{\text{Gly/CB}}(s, k-1) + \text{LLR}_{\text{CCCON}}(s, k-1)$$
   where $p_{\text{mix}}(\delta \mid \mu, \sigma) = 0.78\,\mathcal{N}(\delta \mid \mu, \sigma^2) + 0.22\,\mathcal{N}(\delta \mid \mu, (3.8\sigma)^2)$ absorbs transient IDP secondary-structure excursions (`llr_floor = -5.5`).
   - **3D-Corroborated Noise Rejection (`sys_assign_bonus`)**: When `multi_3d_dataset` is active ($\ge 50\%$ of `CON` roots carry 3D strip peaks), roots corroborated by $\ge 1$ 3D channel receive `b_sys = +2.65`, whereas **3D-unsupported 2D `CON` noise roots (`n_3d_ch == 0`) unconditionally receive `b_sys = -6.5`** (log-prior odds from 3D relay survival $1 - 0.10^3 = 0.999$ vs $< 0.2\%$ for thermal spikes), cleanly leaving spurious thermal/IPAP roots unassigned (`pos[s] = -1`) even when $S + 1 < L$.
2. **Multiplicity-Penalized Sequential Pair Connectivity $P[s_1, s_2]$ (`idp_pair_matrix` / WGSL `compute_idp_pair`)**:
   Links `CON(k)` ($s_1$) to `CON(k+1)` ($s_2$) across two independent covalent pathways:
   - Shared residue-$k$ $\text{C}\alpha$: $CA_k(s_1, \text{curr}) \leftrightarrow CA_k(s_2, \text{prev})$ ($\sigma_{\text{seq,CA}} = 0.085\text{ ppm}$),
   - Forward carbonyl link: $C'_k(s_1, \text{NCACO/CACO}) \leftrightarrow C'_k(s_2, \text{CON root})$ ($\sigma_{\text{seq,C}'} = 0.055\text{ ppm}$).
   Each channel applies an intra-channel candidate-pair multiplicity penalty $-0.18\ln(n_a n_b)\,\text{clamp}(\text{best}/1.5, 0, 1)$, and positive raw sequential bridges $R(s_1, s_2) > 0$ apply a smooth $C^1$ Hermite candidate-multiplicity ambiguity penalty identically on CPU and WebGPU:
   $$P[s_1, s_2] = R(s_1, s_2) - \text{clamp}\!\left(\frac{R(s_1, s_2)}{2.0},\, 0,\, 1\right) w_{\text{mult}} \ln\sqrt{n_{\text{out}}(s_1)\,n_{\text{in}}(s_2)}, \qquad w_{\text{mult}} = 0.35$$
   where $n_{\text{out}}(s_1) = \max\!\big(1, \sum_{u \ne s_1} \psi(R(s_1, u))\big)$ and $n_{\text{in}}(s_2) = \max\!\big(1, \sum_{u \ne s_2} \psi(R(u, s_2))\big)$ with smoothstep $\psi(r) = 3u^2 - 2u^3$, $u = \text{clamp}((r - 2.10)/2.40, 0, 1)$ (excluding 1D single-channel accidents when the partner channel hits `pair_floor = -3.8`).

## 3. IDP Secondary Structure Propensity — `SSP` & Cooperative 4-State `δ2D` (`T11.3`, `crates/nmr-core/src/idp_propensity.rs`, `crates/nmr-core/src/report.rs`)

### 3.1 Unlabelled Reference Offset & Sample pH Sensing
Before computing secondary chemical shifts $\Delta\delta_{k,a} = (\delta^{\text{obs}}_{k,a} - \hat{\delta}^{\text{off}}_a) - \delta^{\text{RC}}_{k,a}(T, \text{pH}_{\text{eff}})$ against `ncIDP` random-coil baselines:
1. **`estimate_global_rc_offsets` (Conformation-Orthogonal Combinations)**: Because `CA` and `CB` shift in **opposite** directions in $\alpha$-helices (`+3.1` vs `-0.45 ppm`) and $\beta$-strands (`-1.5` vs `+2.15 ppm`), the linear combinations $0.48\,\Delta\delta_{\text{CA}} + 0.52\,\Delta\delta_{\text{CB}}$ and $\Delta\delta_{\text{C}'} + 0.72\,\Delta\delta_{\text{CB}}$ cancel conformational bias to first order even in fully folded proteins, recovering `DSS`/`TMS` referencing offsets over interior `[0.15, 0.85]` quantiles with exact permutation invariance (`< 1e-12`).
2. **Unsupervised Clamped Acidic-Sample pH Detector (`infer_effective_ph`)**: When sample pH is unspecified (`sample_ph = None`), evaluates the unlabelled protonated `(Asp⁰, Glu⁰)` `(CA, CB)` tuple fraction (`>= 0.55` after upper-half `[0.45, 0.88]` carrier-offset removal) and `13CB` quantile $L_1$ fit (`pH 2.2` vs `pH 6.5`): neutral/physiological samples return `6.5`, whereas strongly acidic samples map through a clamped protonation-fraction window (`[0.65, 0.96]`) onto $\text{pH}_{\text{eff}} \in [2.72, 3.83]$ (`effective_ph`), preventing false $\alpha$-helical drift on low-pH unfolded depositions (such as `bmr4375` `8 M`-urea Ubiquitin deposited at `pH 2.0`, which hits the `2.72` acidic clamp floor).

### 3.2 Marsh-2006-Inspired Extended 5-Residue Weighted `SSP`-Like Score
Inspired by Marsh et al. (*Protein Sci.* 2006 — which defines unweighted 5-residue `(ΔCA - ΔCB)` averaging with pre-Proline scaling), `nmr-core::idp_propensity` computes an extended multi-nucleus `SSP`-like score across $a \in \{\text{CA}, \text{CB}, \text{C}', \text{HA}, \text{N}, \text{H}^{\text{N}}\}$ at position $j$ (`Gly` omits `CB`; `Pro` omits `H`/`N`; pre-Proline $\text{seq}[j+1] = \text{PRO}$ scales `CA`, `CB`, `C'` by `0.65`). Each per-nucleus secondary shift is divided by residue-dependent $\alpha$-helical ($\Delta\delta^\alpha_{r,a}$) or $\beta$-strand ($\Delta\delta^\beta_{r,a}$) reference shifts, clamped to $[-1.35, +1.35]$, weighted by $w_a = 1 / \sigma_{\text{RC},a}$, and smoothed across a $\pm 2$-residue window (`window_half_width = 2`, positional weights `[1.00, 0.82, 0.52]` for $|j - i| = 0, 1, 2$):
$$\text{SSP}(i) = \text{clamp}\!\left(\frac{\sum_{j=i-2}^{i+2} \omega_{|j-i|} \sum_{a \in \mathcal{O}_j} w_a\, f_{j,a}}{\sum_{j=i-2}^{i+2} \omega_{|j-i|} \sum_{a \in \mathcal{O}_j} w_a},\; -1.25,\; +1.25\right)$$
Positive `SSP` (`-> +1.0`) quantifies fractional $\alpha$-helix formation; negative `SSP` (`-> -1.0`) quantifies extended $\beta$-strand propensity.

### 3.3 Camilloni-2012/`δ2D`-Inspired Cooperative 4-State Forward–Backward HMM Populations
Motivated by Camilloni et al.'s 4-state `δ2D` decomposition (*Biochemistry* 2012 — whereas Camilloni et al.'s original `δ2D` fits reference-set chemical-shift mixtures, our clean-room estimator formulates a 1D linear-chain Hidden Markov Model), each residue $i$ occupies one of four conformational basins $s_i \in \{\alpha, \beta, \text{PPII}, \text{Coil}\}$ (`ConformationalState`) inferred via `compute_idp_propensity`:
1. **Per-State Chemical-Shift Emission Potentials $\phi_i(s)$ (`local_d2d_log_evidence`)**: Evaluates asymmetric-basin Gaussian log-likelihoods (`basin_atom_ll`, gently saturated on the outer folded flank so rigid elements never revert to coil) of sequence-corrected secondary shifts $\Delta\delta_{i,a}$ around calibrated 4-state basin centroids (standard non-`Gly`/non-`Pro`: $\alpha$: `+2.72 CA` [`Gly: +1.10`, `Pro: +1.80`], `-1.14 CB`, `+1.88 C'` [`Gly: +1.10`], `-0.30 HA`, `-0.27 H^N`, `-1.50 N`; $\beta$: `-1.09 CA` [`Gly: -0.64`, `Pro: -0.97`], `+1.50 CB`, `-1.02 C'` [`Gly: -0.72`], `+0.32 HA`, `+0.33 H^N`, `+1.82 N`; `PPII`: `-0.32 CA`, `+0.02 CB`, `-0.36 C'`, `+0.02 HA`, `-0.03 H^N`, `-0.20 N`; `Coil`: `+0.14 CA`, `-0.04 CB`, `+0.07 C'`, `-0.02 HA`, `0.0 H^N/N`), combined with residue-intrinsic conformational baselines (`Pro` enriched in `PPII`; `Pro` preceding `Pro` sterically excluded from $\alpha_R$ per Schimmel & Flory *J. Mol. Biol.* 1968; `Gly` enriched in `Coil`; unassigned residues controlled by `UnassignedPriorMode::{CoilBaseline, Uniform}`).
2. **Cooperative Nearest-Neighbor Forward–Backward Marginals (`forward_backward_d2d`)**: Applies cooperative log-transition matrices (`trans_std` enforcing the $\alpha/\beta$ nucleation barrier vs local `PPII`/`Coil` interconversion, and `trans_into_pro` suppressing $\alpha \to \alpha$ propagation across $i \to \text{Pro}_{i+1}$ for **all** $i+1 \ge 1$, including N-terminal `Pro` at $k = 1, 2$, in both forward and backward passes), solving exact Forward–Backward log-domain marginals normalized via numerically stable `softmax4`:
   $$p_\alpha(i) + p_\beta(i) + p_{\text{PPII}}(i) + p_{\text{coil}}(i) = 1.0 \quad (\text{exact within } \pm 10^{-9}\text{ for every residue } i)$$

   - Selects the target polypeptide chain by maximum sequence alignment (`align_by_res_names`), filters `HETATM` metal / bridging-chalcogen nodes strictly to those coordinated within **`<= 3.8 Å`** of a target-chain donor (`SG`, `SD`/`SE`, `ND1`/`NE2`, `OD*`, `OE*`, backbone `O`) — rejecting crystal-packing symmetry chains even if chain IDs are spoofed — and groups polynuclear cofactors (such as `[2Fe-2S]` and `[4Fe-4S]`) via **`<= 4.5 Å` metal/bridging-chalcogen connected components** so multi-cluster proteins (`2x[4Fe-4S]` bacterial ferredoxins with `~11.5–12 Å` inter-cubane separation) never merge!
   - When `HETATM` cofactors are absent from a `.pdb`, `partition_ligands_3d_single_linkage` clusters true sidechain donor coordinates (`CYS SG`, `HIS ND1/NE2`, `ASP OD*`, `GLU OE*`, `MET/MSE SD/SE`; `CB`/`CA` fallback only on `PdbChain`-only callers via `compute_bleaching_probabilities_from_pdb`) using a **`<= 6.4 Å` donor–donor cutoff**, keeping intra-cluster tetrahedral coordination spheres (`5.5–6.2 Å`) intact while cleanly separating `2x[4Fe-4S]` clusters (`d(SG, SG) >= 6.7 Å`).
   - Across sites $m$ with site-specific $r_{\text{bleach},m} > 0$, per-residue amide bleaching (`compute_bleaching_probabilities_3d_per_site`) is:
     $$p_{\text{bleach}}(k) = \frac{\sum_m (r_{\text{bleach},m} / r_{k,m})^6}{1 + \sum_m (r_{\text{bleach},m} / r_{k,m})^6} \in [0, 1]$$
2. **Sequence-Only Coordination Motif Detection (`detect_metal_coordination_motifs`)**:
   When no 3D `.pdb` is supplied, `paramagnetic.rs` parses explicit `--metal-ligands` (`parse_metal_ligands`, accepting `CYS42`, `C42`, or bare `42`) or automatically scans primary sequence motifs (`MetalMotifKind`: `Cisd2Fe2S`, `FerredoxinFeS`, `Rieske2Fe2S`, `Cys2HisCysFinger`, `Cys2His2Finger`, `RubredoxinOrCys4`). Coordinating histidines (`coordinating_histidine_indices`) automatically set `metal_coordinated_hint = true` in `nmr-core::aromatic::HisTautomerEvidence`.

### 4.2 Paramagnetic-Aware Backbone Optimization (`u_para` Prior, Generic `CA`-Less Filter & Penumbra Corroboration)
`assign_paramagnetic_backbone` and `assign_paramagnetic_from_peaks` (`ParamagneticSolverMode::{Standard, EvoGraph}`) separate two orthogonal mechanisms (independently toggleable via `enable_u_para_bleach_prior` and `enable_generic_ca_less_filter` for controlled ablation):
- **Hard Blind-Sphere Exclusion (`enable_u_para_bleach_prior`)**: Slots with $p_{\text{bleach}}(k) \ge p_{\text{hard}}$ (`hard_bleach_cutoff = 0.85`, corresponding to $r \le 0.749\,r_{\text{bleach}} \approx 6.37\text{ Å}$ at $r_{\text{bleach}} = 8.5\text{ Å}$) receive `FORBID = -1.0e9` unless user-locked.
- **Penumbra Log-Survival Prior (`0.65 <= p_bleach < 0.85`)**: Requires $\ge 2$ positive carbon slots (`FORBID` otherwise) and adds $\Delta U = w_{\text{bleach}} \ln(\max(1 - p_{\text{bleach}}(k), 0.05))$ (`bleach_prior_weight = 2.2`).
- **Generic `CA`-Less Excess/Artifact Root Guard (`enable_generic_ca_less_filter`)**: On triple-resonance datasets (`>= 6` multi-slot systems) with missing-amide slack (`S > N_nonPro`, `S + 3 <= N_nonPro`, or $\sum_k p_{\text{bleach}}(k) \ge 1.5$), forbids `CA`-less partial noise/sidechain roots at non-`Gly`, non-post-`Pro` positions regardless of metal coordinates.
- **Penumbra Walk Corroboration & Diamagnetic Rescue (`penumbra_placement_is_corroborated` / `filter_blind_zones_and_reoptimize`)**: Any tentative placement in the blind-zone penumbra ($0.65 \le p_{\text{bleach}}(k) < 0.85$) is retained **if and only if** (i) $U_{\text{base}}(s, k) \ge 2.5$ with $\ge 2$ positive carbon slots, (ii) when `CB(i-1)` is available (`>= 5` systems) and `seq[k] != GLY`, `CB(k)` survives on $s_{k+1}$ within $1.6\,\sigma_{\text{CB}}$, and (iii) an unbroken sequential walk of **$\ge 2$ links ($\ge 3$ residues) with $P \ge 2.8$ at each step** anchors the placement into the diamagnetic scaffold ($p_{\text{bleach}} < 0.65$). Uncorroborated blind-zone placements abstain cleanly (`-1`), while genuine multi-slot ($\ge 2$ carbon) backbone systems freed from the penumbra are rescued onto vacant diamagnetic slots when $P_{\text{link}} \ge 2.2$ or $U_{\text{para}} \ge 3.0$.

### 4.3 Traceless $\Delta\boldsymbol{\chi}$ Tensor (+ Optional $\delta_{\text{off}}$) SVD, Blind-Sphere PCS Filtering, Standardized Huber IRLS & Heteroscedastic `2·NLL` `VarPro` (`P1-11`)
In anisotropic paramagnetic centers (lanthanides $\text{Tm}^{3+}, \text{Tb}^{3+}, \text{Dy}^{3+}, \text{Yb}^{3+}$, low-spin $\text{Fe}^{3+}$, high-spin $\text{Co}^{2+}$), the point-dipole pseudocontact shift (`PCS`, in ppm) at nuclear displacement $\mathbf{r} = (x, y, z) = \mathbf{x}_{\text{nuc}} - \mathbf{x}_{\text{M}}$ ($r = \|\mathbf{r}\|_2$ in Å, $\Delta\chi$ components in SI $10^{-32}\text{ m}^3$) plus optional constant inter-sample referencing offset $\delta_{\text{off}}$ (`PcsFitOptions::fit_offset`, stored in `DeltaChiTensor::pcs_offset_ppm`; `0.0` by default) is exact linear in the 5 (or 6) parameters:
$$\Delta\delta^{\text{PCS}}(\mathbf{r}) = \frac{10^{4}}{4\pi r^5}\Big[(x^2 - z^2)\,\Delta\chi_{xx} + (y^2 - z^2)\,\Delta\chi_{yy} + 2xy\,\Delta\chi_{xy} + 2xz\,\Delta\chi_{xz} + 2yz\,\Delta\chi_{yz}\Big] + \delta_{\text{off}}$$
1. **Paramagnetic Blind-Sphere & `[Fe-S]` Contact-Shift PCS Exclusion (`filter_pcs_observations_blind_zone` / `fit_pcs_tensor_paramagnetic`, `Q4`)**: Automatically excludes observations inside the core `PRE` blind sphere ($p_{\text{bleach}} \ge \text{hard\_bleach\_cutoff} = 0.85$, e.g. $r \le 6.37\text{ Å}$ at $r_{\text{bleach}} = 8.5\text{ Å}$) or inside the covalent Fermi-contact / point-dipole breakdown shell ($r < 8.0\text{ Å}$) of polynuclear `[Fe-S]` clusters before tensor fitting.
2. **Linear Thin Jacobi SVD & Coordinate-Gradient GLS (`fit_pcs_tensor_svd`, `fit_pcs_tensor_with_options`)**: Validates $N \ge 6$ (`fit_offset = false`) or $N \ge 7$ (`fit_offset = true`, ensuring $\ge 1$ residual DoF), finite coordinates/PCS values, $r \ge 1.2\text{ Å}$, and $\sigma > 0$, solves the $N \times 5$ (or column-equilibrated $N \times 6$) weighted system via generic one-sided Hestenes Jacobi SVD (`solve_nxk_svd::<K>`, `cond <= 1e8`), iterates 2-step analytic coordinate-gradient GLS (`effective_pcs_sigma_ppm`: $\sigma_{\text{eff},i}^2 = \sigma_{\text{pcs},i}^2 + \frac{1}{3}\sigma_{\text{coord},i}^2 \|\nabla_{\mathbf{r}}\Delta\delta^{\text{PCS}}(\mathbf{r}_i)\|_2^2$), diagonalizes $\Delta\boldsymbol{\chi}$ in the Schmitz/Otting `UTS` convention ($|\Delta\chi'_{zz}| \ge |\Delta\chi'_{yy}| \ge |\Delta\chi'_{xx}|$), and reports `ZYZ` Euler angles, `pcs_offset_ppm`, and the Cornilescu $Q$-factor.
3. **Standardized-Residual Huber IRLS (`fit_pcs_tensor_robust`, `PcsFitOptions::huber_k`)**: Standardizes every residual by its propagated effective uncertainty $\sigma_{\text{eff},i}$,
   $$z_i = \frac{|\Delta\delta^{\text{obs}}_i - \Delta\delta^{\text{calc}}_i|}{\sigma_{\text{eff},i}}, \qquad w_{\text{Huber}}(z_i) = \begin{cases} 1, & z_i \le k_{\text{Huber}}, \\ \sqrt{k_{\text{Huber}} / z_i}, & z_i > k_{\text{Huber}}, \end{cases}$$
   preventing high-uncertainty near-metal spins from being double-downweighted when $z_i \le k_{\text{Huber}}$.
4. **8- or 9-Parameter Radius-Bounded Separable Variable-Projection Metal Refinement Under Full Heteroscedastic Gaussian `2·NLL` (`refine_metal_position_and_tensor`, `refine_metal_position_and_tensor_with_options`, `pcs_gaussian_two_nll`, `P1-11`)**: Optimizes $\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}} = (x_0, y_0, z_0) \in \mathbb{R}^3$ ($N \ge 8$ for `fit_offset = false`, $N \ge 9$ for `fit_offset = true`) subject to the strict spherical trust constraint $\|\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}} - \mathbf{x}_{\text{M},0}\|_2 \le R_{\text{search}}$ (`search_radius_angstrom == 0` pins $\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}} = \mathbf{x}_{\text{M},0}$; `search_radius_angstrom < 0` returns `Err`) via coarse 3D grid search followed by barrier-constrained 3D Nelder–Mead simplex minimization (`init_delta <= 0.5 * R_search`) while projecting out $(\boldsymbol{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}}), \delta_{\text{off}}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}}))$ at every step via GLS SVD. Critically, the outer `VarPro` objective evaluates the full heteroscedastic Gaussian twice-negative-log-likelihood (`pcs_gaussian_two_nll`), including the log-normalization penalty $+2\ln\sigma_{\text{eff},i}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}})$:
   $$\mathcal{L}_{2\text{NLL}}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}}, \Delta\boldsymbol{\chi}, \delta_{\text{off}}) = \sum_{i=1}^N \left[\left(\frac{\Delta\delta^{\text{obs}}_i - \Delta\delta^{\text{calc}}_i}{\sigma_{\text{eff},i}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}})}\right)^2 + 2\ln\sigma_{\text{eff},i}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}})\right]$$
   so artificially inflating $\sigma_{\text{eff},i}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}})$ (by pushing $\mathbf{x}_{\text{M}}$ toward uncertain coordinates or inflating $\|\Delta\boldsymbol{\chi}\|$) strictly increases rather than spuriously decreases the objective (`test_varpro_gaussian_two_nll_penalizes_variance_inflation_and_fits_pcs_offset`).

```text

nmrassign assign (--seq <SEQ|seq.fasta> | --star <ref.str>) [--peaks <peaks.tsv>] \

nmrassign pipeline (--seq <SEQ|seq.fasta> (--hsqc <path> | --fid <dir> | --con <path> | --peaks <peaks.tsv>) | --star <entry.str>) \
  [--regime globular|idp-13c|paramagnetic] [--engine auto|gpu|cpu] --out-dir <out/>

nmrassign propensity --shifts <shifts.str|shifts.nef> [--temp-k 298.15] [--ph 6.5] \

# 4. HTML + inline SVG quality report with stacked 4-state d2D + SSP track:
nmrassign report --star|--shifts <shifts.str> [--peaks <peaks.tsv>] [--pdb <model.pdb>] \

```
- `--regime globular` (default) preserves exact backwards-compatible `1H–15N` triple-resonance behavior (`03-assignment.md`).
- `--regime idp-13c` accepts pre-picked `TSV` peak lists or volumetric `13C`-detected spectra (`--con`, `--caco`, `--canco`, `--cbcacon`, `--cccon`, `--ncaco` or auto-classified folders via `pick_c13_idp_dataset`) and dispatches `nmr_gpu::idp_gpu::assign_idp_13c_gpu` / `assign_idp_13c_cpu`: **`--engine gpu` fails with an explicit hard error (zero silent CPU fallback)** if no WebGPU adapter is available or GPU execution errors, `--engine auto` (default) attempts WebGPU and falls back to `assign_idp_13c_cpu` with a loud `stderr` warning, and `--engine cpu` runs directly on CPU, writing continuous through-Proline NMR-STAR v3 / NEF v1.1 shifts and confidences (and automatically embedding the `SSP`/`δ2D` track in `nmrassign pipeline --regime idp-13c`).
- `--regime paramagnetic` dispatches `assign_paramagnetic_from_peaks` (`--solver standard|evo-graph`) with sequence-motif / `--metal-ligands` / `--pdb` Solomon $r_{\text{M}}^{-6}$ `PRE` bleaching, and when `--pcs <pcs.tsv>` and `--pdb <model.pdb>` are supplied, fits and prints the 5-parameter traceless $\Delta\boldsymbol{\chi}$ tensor ($\Delta\chi_{\text{ax}}$, $\Delta\chi_{\text{rh}}$, `ZYZ` Euler angles, and Cornilescu $Q$-factor).

### 5.2 Interactive WebGPU Workbench (`nmr-gui`)
- **Experiment Selection Matrix & Regime Selector**: Auto-populates problem regime (`Globular`, `IDP (13C-Detected)`, `Paramagnetic`) from `nmr-core::classify` (`2D CON` / `CACO` / `CANCO` pulse programs $\to$ `IDP (13C)`, metal/paramagnetic cues $\to$ `Paramagnetic`) with manual override in the top toolbar and guided workflow stepper.
- **2D `CON` Root Map & Continuous Proline Ribbon**: In `idp-13c` mode, renders the `2D CON` `(13C', 15N)` plane (highlighting the `>= 131.5 ppm` `Pro` imino region), walks `3D CANCO` / `CBCACON` / `NCACO` strips uninterrupted through `Pro` tiles on the sequence ribbon, and displays per-residue `(SSP, p_alpha, p_beta, p_ppii, p_coil)` hover/panel readouts; in `paramagnetic` mode, badges `PRE`-bleached coordination loops on the sequence ribbon so analysts immediately distinguish physical blind zones from missing data.
